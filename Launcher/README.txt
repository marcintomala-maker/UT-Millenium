UT MILLENIUM - Quick Play for Unreal Tournament (1999)
======================================================
Pick EASY / MEDIUM / HARD and you're in a match. One match, then back to the menu.

WHAT YOU NEED
- Unreal Tournament GOTY installed (the Steam version works out of the box).
- Windows 10 or 11, and internet the first time (UT Millenium downloads the
  free OldUnreal 469e update for Unreal Tournament, about 101 MB).

HOW TO START
1. If you downloaded the zip: right-click it > Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK.
   (Otherwise Windows blocks the launcher.)
2. Unzip it anywhere (the Desktop is fine).
3. Double-click "UT Millenium Launcher".
4. The first time it finds Unreal Tournament and asks you to confirm (press Y).
   It sets everything up inside this "UT Millenium Data" folder.
   Your Steam installation is only read, never changed.
5. Play! Next time just double-click "UT Millenium Launcher" again, or the
   "UT Millenium" shortcut (with the UT icon) it puts on your Desktop.

IN THE MENU
- EASY / MEDIUM / HARD: random game mode, map and bots.
  Easy = Novice to Experienced bots, Medium = Skilled to Masterful,
  Hard = Inhuman and Godlike. Each bot gets its own random difficulty.
- Casual Move Speed (on by default): everybody moves 10% slower.
- Max Bots: the map is filled with bots up to its maximum.
- Just Two Teams: Team Deathmatch and Domination always use two teams
  (otherwise 2, 3 or 4).
- ESC during a match: menu (game paused). ESC again: back to the game.
  QUIT MATCH leaves the match (it doesn't count as a win or a loss).
- ADD MAPS: put downloaded maps (.unr, .zip or folders) into the
  "Add Maps" folder that opens, then press a key.
- F9 in the game saves a screenshot into UT Millenium Data\Game\System.

KEYS (change them in Settings > Key Bindings)
- W A S D move, Space jump, C crouch, mouse to aim and shoot.
- 1 - 0 pick weapons (as in the original), Q translocator, F1 scores.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- UT Millenium tells you in plain words. Details are in launcher.log here.
- To set everything up again from scratch, delete the "Game" folder in here
  and start the launcher again.
