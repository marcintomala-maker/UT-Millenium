UT MILLENIUM - Quick Play for Unreal Tournament (1999)
======================================================
Pick EASY / MEDIUM / HARD and you're in a match. One match, then back to the menu.

WHAT YOU NEED
- Unreal Tournament GOTY (1999) installed: the Steam or GOG version
  (a CD install works too).
- Windows 10 or 11.
- Internet the first time: UT Millenium downloads the free OldUnreal 469e
  update for Unreal Tournament (about 101 MB, from github.com/OldUnreal).
  It's the popular community patch that keeps UT99 running on modern
  Windows, made by the OldUnreal team. If your Unreal Tournament already
  has 469e installed, nothing is downloaded.
  No internet? Download OldUnreal-UTPatch469e-Windows-x86.zip yourself from
  github.com/OldUnreal/UnrealTournamentPatches (release v469e), put it into
  your Downloads folder and start UT Millenium.

HOW TO START
1. If you downloaded the zip: right-click it > Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK.
   (Otherwise Windows blocks the launcher.)
2. Unzip it anywhere (the Desktop is fine).
3. Double-click "UT Millenium Launcher".
4. The first time it finds Unreal Tournament (it asks Steam and GOG, then
   checks the usual folders on drives C to H) and asks you to confirm (Y).
   If it can't find it, drag your Unreal Tournament folder into the window.
   It sets everything up inside this "UT Millenium Data" folder.
   Your Unreal Tournament installation is only read, never changed.
5. Play! Next time just double-click "UT Millenium Launcher" again, or the
   "UT Millenium" shortcut (with the UT icon) it puts on your Desktop.
   A small LOADING window shows while the game starts and closes by itself
   when the menu appears.

IN THE MENU
- EASY / MEDIUM / HARD: random game mode, map and bots.
  Easy = Novice to Experienced bots, Medium = Skilled to Masterful,
  Hard = Inhuman and Godlike. Each bot gets its own random difficulty.
- Casual Move Speed (on by default): everybody moves 10% slower.
- Max Bots (on by default): the map is filled with bots up to its maximum.
- Just Two Teams (on by default): Team Deathmatch and Domination always use
  two teams (when it's off: 2, 3 or 4).
- Your team is the one picked in Settings > Character (Red if it's None),
  and your teammates come from your character's own faction (wear an
  Iron Guard skin and you fight alongside the Iron Guard).
  Green and Gold only exist in Team Deathmatch and Domination with Just Two
  Teams off: there you always get them; otherwise Green and Gold play on Red.
  Characters without a faction (Xan, for example) get a random team.
- Before each match a briefing board shows the game mode, your team,
  the enemies, the objectives and the map. Click or press a key to start.
- ESC during a match: menu (game paused). ESC again: back to the game.
  QUIT MATCH leaves the match (it doesn't count as a win or a loss).
- ADD MAPS: put downloaded maps (.unr, .zip or folders) into the
  "Add Maps" folder that opens, then press a key.
- F9 in the game saves a screenshot into UT Millenium Data\Game\System.
- SETTINGS: brightness, field of view, frame rate limit, HUD size, mouse
  sensitivity (default 1.5), volumes, key bindings, HUD and crosshair (the
  original UT page: HUD colors, crosshair style and color), character (drag the 3D preview to rotate it, mouse
  wheel to zoom in on the face), game modes and maps, mutators (hover one to
  read what it does), auto taunt.
- MUTATORS (Settings > Choose Mutators), besides the original UT ones:
  Chainsaw Zombies / Impact Hammer Zombies (enemy bots only carry that
  weapon and hunt you down), One Man Army (team games: everyone against
  you), Bullet Time (every kill slows time down for 2.5 seconds).
- PLAY INTRO / PLAY OUTRO: the original intro and the champion's ending.

KEYS (change them in Settings > Key Bindings)
- W A S D move, Space jump, C crouch, mouse to aim and shoot.
- 1 - 0 pick weapons (as in the original), Q translocator.
- Hold Tab for the scoreboard. T or Enter to chat, Y to chat with your team.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
- UT Millenium tells you in plain words. Details are in launcher.log here.
- To set everything up again from scratch, delete the "Game" folder in here
  and start the launcher again.
